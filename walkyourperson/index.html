Help

Questions from grown-ups

How do I open the grown-up settings?

In the person maker, tap the small "For grown-ups" button. Answer the multiplication question (or enter your password if you set one).

How do I set a daily play-time limit?

Open the grown-up settings and choose Play time each day: No limit, 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. Time only counts while the game is on screen and starts fresh each day. When time is up, a grown-up can add 10 more minutes, change settings, or keep the break.

I forgot my password.

On the password screen, tap "Forgot your password?" and answer the harder multiplication question. The password is turned off; nothing else is lost.

There's no sound.
  1. Check the sound button at the top of the game: it switches between Loud, Quiet and Off.
  2. Turn up the device volume with the side buttons.
  3. Make sure no headphones or Bluetooth speaker are connected.
How do I change the voice or accent?

Open the grown-up settings and pick a Voice accent and Voice (woman or man). Tap ▶ to hear a sample.

What does "Download latest voices" do?

It checks friendslog.net for new or better voices and new languages, shows the download size, and only downloads if you say so. The voices that came with the app always keep working. Nothing about your child is sent.

How do I start over?

At the bottom of the grown-up settings, tap "Delete all data", then "Yes, delete everything". This removes people, lands, friends' names, settings, the password, play time and downloaded voices. Deleting the app does the same.
