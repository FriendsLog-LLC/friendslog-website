1. Who we are

Walk Your Person is a game for young children for iPhone and iPad, developed and published by FriendsLog LLC ("we", "us" or "our"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use Walk Your Person.

2. What we collect

Nothing. Walk Your Person does not collect, store on our servers, use or share any personal information, from children or from adults. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. It contains no third-party code that collects data.

The app never asks for the microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or any other permission. There is no typing anywhere in the game, so children cannot enter their name or any other information.

3. What stays on your device

To let your child keep playing where they left off, the game saves a few things on the device itself. We cannot see or reach any of it.

WhatWhy
People your child made, made-up lands and the names given to friendsSo they are still there next time
Grown-up settings: voice accent and voice, sound level, play-time limit, the optional 4-number password, the grown-up question settingSo your choices are remembered
Today's play timeFor the daily play-time limit; it starts fresh each day
Downloaded voicesSo new voices work without the internet

The password is a child lock for the grown-up settings, not a security feature. Don't use a number you use for anything else.

4. When the app goes online

Every picture and sound is inside the app, so it works with no internet connection. The app goes online only when a grown-up opens the settings (behind the grown-up question or password) and taps Download latest voices. It then downloads a list of voices and, if you agree, voice sound files from our website, friendslog.net.

The app sends nothing about your child with these downloads. Like any website, the server that hosts friendslog.net (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.) receives the device's internet address with each request and may keep a record of it for security. We do not receive, use or store these records. See GitHub's privacy statement.

5. Third parties

  • Apple sells and delivers the app through the App Store. Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy; we do not receive personal information about buyers from Apple.
  • GitHub hosts friendslog.net, including the voice downloads (see section 4).

We do not sell, rent or share information with anyone, because we don't have any.

6. Children's privacy

Walk Your Person is made for children age 5 and under. We do not collect personal information from children, as described in the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws elsewhere.

  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no links to websites or other apps, and no social features.
  • Settings and voice downloads sit behind a grown-up gate (a multiplication question or a password).
  • A grown-up can set a daily play-time limit.

If you think information about a child has reached us anyway, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Deleting your data

All of the game's data lives on your device. To remove it, open the grown-up settings and tap Delete all data, or delete the app. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes everything it saved.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or CCPA give you rights over your personal information. Because we hold no personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete or stop selling. You are always welcome to ask us questions.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date. If a change ever affects what the app does with information, we will say so in the app update notes before it takes effect.
